# May two tests follow the rename of the page-object method they call?

## Question
Two rows the story closes rename a method of the browser suite's page objects: `LoginPage.RegisterLinkTargetAsync` → `RegisterLinkPathAsync` (login-leads-only-to-shop-pages#1) and `BasePage.NoticesAsync` → `AccountNoticesAsync` (registration-says-the-account-was-created#3). Each is called inside an assertion line of a test that existed before the task — `tests/DcaShop.E2eTests/LoginE2eTest.cs:75` and `tests/DcaShop.E2eTests/RegistrationE2eTest.cs:36` and `:50` — and the gate's `tests-kept` reads a changed assertion line as a changed expectation, which only a person's word backs: the story's `## Changed expectations`, which a task has none of, or a decision. The expected values (`/register`, "Account created successfully! Welcome!") and the selectors do not change. The tree keeps the old names for now; the other nine rows are built.

## Options
- a: The two tests follow the rename. This answer backs the plan's `## Changed tests` rows, and the next round renames the two methods and their three call sites.
- b: The two rename rows leave this task — taken out of its `closes:` — and stay open for a story that changes those tests anyway.
- c: The two rows are set to wont-fix and the old names stay.

## Recommendation
a — the rename changes no expected value and no selector, it is exactly what the two findings ask for, and a decision is the gate's own way to back a changed test line.
