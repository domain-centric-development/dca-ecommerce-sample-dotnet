# The main line's three test-helper lines call APIs this story replaced — may the integration keep them rewritten, or must they come back verbatim?

## Question
The runner's `main-tests` check sent the integration back: three test files lost lines the main line holds, and the
prompt asks to put them back verbatim beside the story's, editing nothing else. None of the three lines compiles against
the merged code:

- `tests/DcaShop.UnitTests/Checkout/CheckoutCartSnapshotAccessTest.cs` and
  `tests/DcaShop.UnitTests/Checkout/CheckoutSessionTest.cs` (`ConfirmWith`): main calls
  `pricing.CalculateOrderTotal(session, facts)` and `session.Confirm(validation, subtotal, authorized)` with an authorized
  amount of `session.Totals.Total`. The story removed `CheckoutPricing.CalculateOrderTotal` (no occurrence left in `src/`)
  and `CheckoutSession.Confirm` now takes `(validation, facts, shown fingerprint, authorizedPayment)`
  (`src/DcaShop.Checkout/Domain/Model/CheckoutSession.cs:150`) and refuses a payment below today's total.
- `tests/DcaShop.UnitTests/Specification/CheckoutSpecificationTest.cs` (`Confirm(CheckoutSession)`): main builds
  `new ConfirmCheckoutCommand(sessionId, customerId)`; the command now requires the fingerprint
  (`src/DcaShop.Checkout/Application/CheckoutCompletion/ConfirmCheckout/ConfirmCheckoutCommand.cs:4`). A one-argument
  overload beside the story's `Confirm(session, shown = null)` would also be ambiguous.

The lines are helper lines, not test cases: every main-line `[Fact]` and vector case that uses them is still there and
runs through the merged helper. The merged helpers keep main's shape (authorize, then confirm with the payment from
`InMemoryPaymentProviderRegistry([new StandIn()])`) and only adapt the call to the new signatures (see `integrate.md`).

## Options
- a: Accept the rewritten helpers as the resolution: the three lines stay replaced by their merged form, and the
  `main-tests` check is satisfied by this record for these three lines. Cost: the check's literal rule is waived once.
- b: Restore the three lines verbatim beside the story's. Cost: the unit test project no longer compiles; the build
  stage would then have to bring back `CalculateOrderTotal`, the old `Confirm` overload and a fingerprint-less command,
  which contradicts the story (decision 03: "Place Order" always carries the fingerprint).
- c: Restore them as comments next to the merged lines. Cost: satisfies a line check without being code — the check is
  evaded rather than answered.

## Recommendation
a — the main line's behaviour (authorize at Place Order, the payment provider registry, every main test case) holds
in the merged helpers; only the calls moved to the signatures the story introduced. b breaks the build and the story,
c is not recommended.
