# The cart page already shows today's price and the change notice — adopt the story instead of building it?

## Question
`the-cart-shows-todays-price-after-a-change` (happy path) holds on today's code, now that `an-operator-changes-a-price`
is delivered, so the test gate would refuse its test as green:

- the line's "Unit Price" is the current price: `i.Article.CurrentPrice.ToString()`
  (`src/DcaShop.Cart/Adapter/Incoming/Web/Shopping/CartPageController.cs:160`);
- "Price decreased by 5.00 EUR": `HasPriceChanged`, `PriceIncreased`, `PriceDifference` compare the current price with
  the price at addition (`src/DcaShop.Cart/Domain/Model/EnrichedCartItem.cs:14-19`), rendered at
  `src/DcaShop.Web/Views/Cart/View.cshtml:39-48`; `Money.ToString` writes "5.00 EUR"
  (`src/DcaShop.SharedKernel/Domain/Model/Money.cs:49`);
- "Some items have price changes since they were added to your cart.": `EnrichedCart.HasAnyPriceChanges`
  (`src/DcaShop.Cart/Domain/Model/EnrichedCart.cs:49`), rendered at `src/DcaShop.Web/Views/Cart/View.cshtml:18-21`;
- the staff change reaches the price the cart reads: `PUT /api/products/<id>/price`
  (`tests/DcaShop.IntegrationTests/PriceChangeTest.cs:76-79`).

Only code carries it: no delivered or adopted story names the scenario, no spec line
(`product/spec/cart/shopping/scenarios.md` has none), and no test asserts the notice or the changed line
(`price-change-alert` / `price-change-summary` appear in no test under `tests/`). The story's own note foresaw this
("Where the plan finds it green then, it recommends adopting").

## Options
- a: The story becomes `adopted`: the plan maps the scenario to where it lives, the test stage writes the browser test
  as a characterization test with a break, and the spec gains the key; no production code changes.
- b: The story is superseded: the behaviour stays as it is without a scenario or a test of its own.
- c: The story is changed to ask for something the cart page does not do yet (other wording, another placement).

## Recommendation
a — the behaviour is wanted (Q4.7) and works, but nothing asserts it; adoption gives it its spec line and its test,
as the story's note asks, and builds nothing.
