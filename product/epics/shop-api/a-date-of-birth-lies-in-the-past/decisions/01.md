# Two criteria the story carries already hold today — keep them in the story, or take them out?

## Question

The story carries `unreadable-date-is-refused` and `future-date-is-refused` word for word, only because it renames their
rule (story `## Notes`). Both already hold on today's code and have their tests:
`tests/DcaShop.IntegrationTests/RegistrationPageTest.cs:179` (`ADateOfBirthThatIsNotADateIsRefused`) and
`tests/DcaShop.IntegrationTests/RegistrationPageTest.cs:187` (`ADateOfBirthInTheFutureIsRefused`), named by
`product/spec/account/registration/scenarios.md` under "The date of birth must be a real date, and not in the future".
The test gate refuses both (`tests-red`): a test green before the build proves nothing for the story, and break
patches do not count outside a round the judge sent back. Should they stay in the story?

## Options
- a: Take both scenarios out of the story; it keeps the two new ones and the rule's new words.
  The spec keeps both scenarios with their tests; the document stage moves them under the renamed rule. Costs a check
  that the spec delta accepts a renamed rule whose other criteria the story does not carry.
- b: Keep both in the story and let the pipeline accept a carried criterion that holds word for word.
  Needs a change to the test gate in the pipeline first; this story waits for it.
- c: Leave the rule's old words and put the two new scenarios under a new rule of their own, "The date of birth must lie in the past".
  No carried criteria; the spec then holds two date rules, the old one still saying "not in the future".

## Recommendation
a — the behaviour of both scenarios already has its story and its tests, a second claim adds nothing; the story stays
about today's date, which is what it changes.

## Answer
