# Three scenarios expect a request or a sentence the cart API does not have. Change the story, or build the difference?

## Question
The story is `status: adopted`, so it describes behaviour the shop already has. Status and title match the code in
every scenario. Three scenarios do not match in full:

| Scenario | Story expects | The shop today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| an-unknown-product-is-not-available | `productId` `"no-such-product"` answers 404 "Article not available" | `productId` is a `Guid` (`src/DcaShop.Cart/Adapter/Incoming/Api/AddToCartRequest.cs:6`): that body is refused by model binding with 400. An unknown **Guid** answers 404, title and detail "There is no article for product {productId}." as the story says (`CartApiExceptionHandler.cs:61`; `tests/DcaShop.IntegrationTests/ApiProblemDetailTest.cs:75` does exactly that) |
| more-than-in-stock-is-refused | detail "Insufficient stock for product: {id}" | detail "Requested 9 of product {id}, more than is in stock." (`src/DcaShop.Cart/Adapter/Incoming/Api/CartApiExceptionHandler.cs:72`); the story's sentence is the exception's log message (`src/DcaShop.Cart/Application/Shopping/AddItemToCart/InsufficientArticleStockException.cs:16`) |
| removing-a-product-not-in-the-cart | detail "Product not found in cart: {id}" | detail "The cart holds no position for {id}." (`CartApiExceptionHandler.cs:66`); the story's sentence is the log message (`src/DcaShop.Cart/Domain/Model/CartItemNotFoundException.cs:30`) |

`docs/architecture/refusal-wording.md:28-30` fixes the shop's details, the same in both shops. The precedent: decision
`registration-refuses-an-unusable-form-01` — "an adopted story describes what the shop does", decided for every
replay story at once (WP-107, 2026-10-12). This story's text was not changed then.

exactly-the-stock-is-accepted and a-quantity-of-zero-is-refused match today (`AddToCartRequest.cs:7` gives 400).

An adoption changes no production code, and a plan does not rewrite the story's text, so the plan stage stopped.

## Options
- a: Change the story: the unknown product is sent as an id no product has (a fresh Guid), and the two details read
  "Requested 9 of product <id of Wooden Hexagon Desk Model>, more than is in stock." and "The cart holds no
  position for <id of Team Topologies>." It stays adopted; nothing in production changes.
- b: Make it a story to build: accept a non-Guid product id with 404 and word the two details as the story says.
  Changes `refusal-wording.md` in both shops, the Java sample and the shared specification, and puts the
  exception's log message back into the problem document.
- c: Take the three scenarios out; adopt the two that match.

## Recommendation
Option a — it follows the WP-107 decision and the shared wording in `refusal-wording.md`.

## Answer
answer: a for the two details — the story now reads the shops' sentences ("Requested 9 of product <id>, more than is in stock.", "The cart holds no position for <id>."), changed in both shops and the replay source; for the unknown product the Java shop is right: a change story, cart-api-takes-any-product-id, brings the .NET API in line, and this story depends on it. It stays adopted.
